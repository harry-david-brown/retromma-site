Support

Found a bug, lost a fight you're sure you won, or just want to say hi? Email harrydavidbrown@icloud.com. Tell me which device you're on, and a screenshot always helps.

How do I fight?

Tap to strike: jab, cross, and hold the last beat for a power hook. Swipe to slip his punches, then hit back while he's open.

Does the game save?

Yes, automatically, after every fight. Your career lives on your device only.

How do I start a new career?

Choose New Game on the title screen. The game asks before it replaces your current fighter.

Can I turn off vibration or the crowd?

Yes. Settings has a switch for haptics and a crowd volume setting.

Will my career carry over to a new phone?

Your career is stored in the app on your device. It moves with an iCloud or computer backup of your iPhone or iPad; deleting the app deletes it.
