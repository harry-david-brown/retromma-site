Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026

RetroMMA does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the game never connects to the internet.

What the game stores

RetroMMA saves two files on your device, inside the app's own private storage:

  • your career (your fighter, record, rankings and progress), and
  • your settings (haptics, crowd volume and your chosen skin).

These files never leave your device. Nobody else can see them, including us. Deleting the app deletes them.

Third parties

The game contains no third-party services, advertising or analytics code. If you have chosen to share diagnostics with Apple in your device settings, Apple may send crash reports to developers; that data is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy and contains no personal information from the game.

Children

Because RetroMMA collects no data from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
